## I. FFS Medi-Cal Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEM) Reimbursement Protocol

**Legal Mandate**: Medi-Cal beneficiaries with Fee-for-Service (FFS) are eligible for reimbursement of mileage, lodging, and/or meals for medically necessary appointments when other transportation means are exhausted and the service is not available locally. Pre-authorization is required for lodging and meals.

**Source Document**: [DHCS FFS Transportation Reimbursement Form (PDF)](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/FFS-NMT-Member-Reimbursement-Form-MILEAGE-LODGING-MEALS.pdf)

**Key Steps for Reimbursement (as per DHCS Form):**
1.  **Obtain the Form**: Download from the DHCS website or call the Beneficiary Service Center at (916) 403-2007.
2.  **Complete the Form**: Use blue/black ink, ensure legibility, and provide an original signature. Include a photocopy of your Benefits Identification Card (BIC).
3.  **Appointment Information (Section IV)**: Complete for each appointment. The Medi-Cal provider *must sign* the Appointment Verification Form (Section IV.A).
4.  **Receipts**: Provide ORIGINAL itemized receipts for lodging and meals (if applicable), showing proof of payment. Mileage does not require receipts.
5.  **Payee Data Record (STD 204 Form)**: Complete, sign, and date this form.
6.  **Submission**: Mail the completed form and all required documents to:
    *   Beneficiary Service Center
    *   P.O. Box 138008
    *   Sacramento, CA 95813-8008
7.  **Timeline**: Submit within one month of the appointment. Response within 60 days.

**Escalation for Non-Compliance**: If DHCS fails to provide services or discriminates, contact the Office of Civil Rights at **1-916-440-7370** or [CivilRights@dhcs.ca.gov](mailto:CivilRights@dhcs.ca.gov).

## III. In-Hospital Needs & Protocols (for all departments: GI, Radiology, Anesthesiology, etc.)

**Legal Mandate**: All hospital departments are legally mandated to adhere to the patient's specific genomic and clinical protocols to prevent iatrogenic harm. Failure to implement these protocols constitutes a patient safety violation and medical negligence.

**Source Documents**:
*   [Bianchini UCLA Genomic Protocol.docx](/home/ubuntu/projects/medical-crisis-risk-mediation-an-04fe68c5/Bianchini UCLA Genomic Protocol.docx)
*   [Brandy_Bianchini_Clinical_Directive.pdf](/home/ubuntu/projects/medical-crisis-risk-mediation-an-04fe68c5/Brandy_Bianchini_Clinical_Directive.pdf)

**Mandatory In-Hospital Protocols:**
1.  **Procedural Safety Firewall**: All pre-meds must be administered IV.
    *   **Immediate Pre-load (25m prior)**: 25mg IV Benadryl + 20mg IV Famotidine.
    *   **Metabolic Rescue**: Post-procedural IV NAC and Methyl-B12 infusion.
2.  **Lactated Ringer's Protocol**: Minimum 1-hour full infusion. Must be administered 20 minutes pre-procedure, continued during imaging (45-60 minutes minimum), and continued post-procedure for monitoring.
3.  **Medication Burn Rate**: Providers must be aware of:
    *   Benadryl: Burns through in ~45-60 minutes.
    *   Diazepam: Faster clearance than standard population.
    *   H1/H2 blockers: Oral absorption only 5-15% due to GI dysfunction.
    *   Albuterol: Administer 1-2 hours before procedure.
4.  **Contrast Toxicity Management**: Not an allergy, but metabolic poisoning. Requires pre-procedure supplementation (methylfolate, B12, NAC, glutathione) and post-procedure monitoring/supplementation.
5.  **Imaging Coordination**: All previous imaging (MRI, CT, X-rays, trans-echo) must be pulled together for interpretation by radiologists. Radiological intervention required for interpretation and coordination.
6.  **Staffing/Monitoring**: Nurse MUST stay with patient during AND after imaging sequence. Vitals must be monitored continuously. Post-procedure crisis is likely if not properly monitored.
7.  **Immunostain Testing**: For PMS2 tumor suppressor, required BEFORE any biopsies.
8.  **Specific Procedures to be Ordered**: EGD, colonoscopy, pancreas biopsy with ultrasound guidance, tissue testing for biopsies, chest mass tissue removal testing, echocardiograms, neurological impairment testing, neurotransmitter testing, neurohormone testing, hormone testing.

## IV. Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to adhere to these mandated protocols, referral requirements, and reimbursement procedures will result in immediate escalation to regulatory bodies and legal action, including but not limited to:
*   **California Medical Board**: For provider negligence and failure to follow established protocols.
*   **DHCS**: For denial of medically necessary care and reimbursement failures.
*   **CMS/HHS Office for Civil Rights**: For discrimination under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).
*   **The Joint Commission**: For patient safety violations and accreditation breaches.
*   **Legal Counsel**: For medical malpractice and other civil actions.
