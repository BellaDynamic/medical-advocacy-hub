# Multi-System Coverage & Evidence Ledger

**Purpose.** This ledger is the working source-control document for the revised website. It prevents a requested test, an advocacy assertion, a self-reported history, or an AI-generated draft from being displayed as a clinician-confirmed diagnosis or treatment direction. It covers the records currently accessible in the project and the attachments supplied in this chat through `Pasted_content_36.txt`.

> **Clinical and legal review required.** This is an AI-organized evidence ledger, not a diagnosis, treatment plan, eligibility determination, legal finding, or proof of causation. A treating clinician must confirm medical decisions, and an attorney or advocate must review any filing or formal grievance.

## Evidence-status key

| Status | Meaning | Website treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Charted result / study metadata** | A value, order, or study label is visible in a chart excerpt or image metadata. | Display as a dated record with its limits. |
| **Original report needed** | The current material references a genetic, imaging, laboratory, or pathology report that is not itself visible in the accessible sources. | Display as a verification request only. |
| **Patient-reported or advocacy source** | The source states a history, symptom, mechanism, request, or allegation in patient/advocacy language. | Preserve as a source statement, not a clinical conclusion. |
| **External educational screening** | A non-diagnostic educational tool or eligibility screener. | Keep separate from diagnostic evidence. |

## Website build rules derived from this ledger

1. Every system page will show **evidence status**, **source filename**, **owner specialty**, and **what needs confirmation**.
2. The site will retain patient advocacy requests but label them **“requested clinical review”** rather than “mandatory care” or a legal conclusion.
3. Medication, infusion, contrast, imaging, surgical, and hormone content will be described as **questions for the treating team** unless a current signed clinician instruction is supplied.
4. The new site structure will show all required domains: immune/BMT, hematology, bone, vascular/heart, kidney, liver, lung, mixed connective tissue, endocrine/mineral, metabolic/detoxification, brain/neuro, GI, oncology, radiology, and ophthalmology.
5. The source vault will preserve original filenames and distinguish chart excerpts, legal/advocacy material, general guidance, and original reports.
