## Lawyer referral services (state-certified, low-cost initial consult)

- **Sacramento County Bar Association Lawyer Referral Service** —
  (916) 564-6707. Covers Marshall Medical Center (Placerville), Sutter,
  Mercy/Dignity, and UC Davis. Administrative fee around $50; first
  30 minutes with the attorney is typically free.
- **LACBA SmartLaw (Los Angeles County Bar Association Lawyer Referral &
  Information Service)** — 1-866-762-7852 (1-866-SMARTLAW), smartlaw.org.
  Covers UCLA. Free to access; no fee for the referral itself.
- **State Bar of California — Find a Lawyer Referral Service** directory,
  if you want a different county or a specialty search:
  https://www.calbar.ca.gov/public/find-legal-professionals/find-lawyer-referral-service

Ask each one specifically for attorneys who handle **medical malpractice
against public university hospitals** and **genetic/rare-disease
misdiagnosis cases** — that combination is unusual enough that the intake
person may need to search a bit; be specific rather than just saying
"medical malpractice."

## UC claims are NOT filed like a normal government claim

Important correction for the attorney handoff summary: the Regents of the
University of California are **exempt** from the standard California
Government Claims Act filing process (Government Code § 905.6). That means
the usual "six-month government claim deadline" advice doesn't apply the
same way to UCLA or UC Davis — claims against UC go directly to the
campus's own Risk Management office, not through the general state Government
Claims Program. This is exactly where the UCLA Risk Management letter
already drafted (`UCLA_RISK_MANAGEMENT_LETTER_DRAFT.md`) was headed — good,
it was aimed at the right office. A parallel letter to **UC Davis Health
Risk Management** (health.ucdavis.edu/risk-management) covering the June
2025 sedation incident and the stalled genomic workup would follow the same
pattern.

UC Legal / Office of the General Counsel (for anything that escalates past
campus Risk Management): (510) 987-9800 — 1111 Franklin Street, 8th Floor,
Oakland, CA 94607.

**An attorney still needs to confirm what limitations period applies** —
UC's exemption from the Government Claims Act doesn't mean there's no
deadline, just that it's a different one. Don't rely on this note alone to
calculate a filing date.

## Disability rights / Medi-Cal / public benefits

- **Disability Rights California** — 1-800-776-5746,
  disabilityrightsca.org. This is California's federally designated
  protection-and-advocacy agency. They've litigated against DHCS directly
  over failure to provide medically necessary services, and they advise on
  legal/civil/service rights for free — this is a real fit for the
  care-coordination and Medi-Cal-access side of your situation, separate
  from the malpractice claims.
- **National Health Law Program** — healthlaw.org — policy and advocacy
  organization focused on health rights for low-income people; not a direct
  representation service but a good source for understanding your rights
  and finding the right advocate.
