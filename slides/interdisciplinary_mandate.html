Required Coordination Protocol

All diagnostic and therapeutic interventions are locked down pending completed mapping against the Genomic Safety Firewall. Treating any manifestation in isolation constitutes clinical reductionism and a failure of care coordination.

UNIFIED CARE PLANNING: Every department must cross-verify any proposed drug or intervention against the Mechanism Matrix prior to care authorization. No siloed decision-making is permitted.
