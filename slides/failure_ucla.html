  • Patient Experience office unresponsiveness: Repeated failure to return calls for 1 year
  • Referral suppression: Specialty referrals blocked despite clinical indication

Downstream Harm

  • EHR erasure: Baseline genomic and imaging data from 2017 inaccessible
  • Accelerated structural degradation from excessive contrast exposures
  • Delayed diagnosis of progressive aortic tortuosity and vascular changes

Required Remediation

  • Immediate EHR restoration of all archived baseline profiles and genomic data
  • Mandatory placement of high-level "Genomic Complexity Warning" in EHR
  • Establishment of formal care coordination with dedicated coordinator
  • Mandatory genomic competency training for all clinical staff
