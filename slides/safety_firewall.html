The Safety Firewall: Mandatory Pre-Procedure Protocol

Mandatory Pre-Procedure Checklist

Genomic Safety Review: Confirm no contraindicated agents will be used

Mechanism Mapping: Document exact pathway of intervention interaction

Pre-Procedure Labs: CMP, CBC, Mg/K levels, coagulation panel, LFTs

Care Coordinator Contact: Notify Dr. Eby's team 48 hours before procedure

Rescue Protocol Review: Ensure staff familiar with neuro-crash recognition

Absolutely Contraindicated Agents

Propofol: Triggers catastrophic GPCR signaling collapse

Fentanyl & Opioids: Absolute methylation pathway blockade

Gadolinium Contrast: Toxic accumulation due to impaired clearance

Iodine-Based Contrast: Acute metabolic and neuro-immune collapse

PEG 3350: Guaranteed electrolyte and PTH axis collapse
