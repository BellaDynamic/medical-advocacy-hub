Legal Mandate & ADA Compliance

ADA Title III: Mandatory Accommodations

  • Effective Communication: Clinical staff must integrate genomic protocols into all care planning to ensure effective communication of risks.
  • Reasonable Modification: Institutions must modify standard protocols (e.g., anesthesia, radiology) to accommodate the patient's genetic impairments.

Institutional Accountability

  • Civil Rights Violation: Failure to provide care coordination for FFS genetic cases constitutes a direct violation of civil rights and ADA protections.
  • Professional Malpractice: Proceeding with standard care after receiving this mandate constitutes gross negligence and malpractice.

MANDATORY COMPLIANCE: All providers and institutions are hereby formally notified that non-compliance with this genomic safety mandate will result in immediate escalation to the HHS Office for Civil Rights and the California Department of Public Health.
