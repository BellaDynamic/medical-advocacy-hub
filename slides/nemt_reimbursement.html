NEMT Reimbursement & Care Entitlements

Medi-Cal NEMT Member Entitlements

  • Mileage Reimbursement: At current DHCS approved rates
  • Lodging: Up to approved daily per-diem for patient and one caregiver
  • Meals: Daily allowance for patient and caregiver during travel
  • TAR/PCS: Mandatory pre-authorization for all multi-trip sequences

Required Documentation for Filing

  • DHCS Form 6065 (Mileage/Lodging/Meals Reimbursement)
  • Geographical Exemption Code (GEC) for 50+ mile travel
  • Verification of Attendance: Multi-trip signature and stamp required

MANDATORY: Providers must provide a physical stamp and signature for every trip. Failure to provide this documentation constitutes a direct barrier to patient care and reimbursement.
