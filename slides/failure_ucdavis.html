Institutional Failures: UC Davis Medical Center

Documented Failure

Anesthesia team proceeded with standard sedation protocols despite documented STX16/PHP1b and COMT/MTHFR variants. Propofol was administered despite clear contraindication.

Downstream Harm

  • Severe metabolic collapse and acute neuro-immune crash
  • Uncontrolled muscle tremors and toxic tissue accumulation
  • Discharge without proper stabilization or rescue response

Required Remediation

  • Formal medical board reporting and incident investigation
  • Mandatory genomic literacy training for all anesthesia staff
  • Implementation of EHR flags for high-complexity genetic conditions
