09

Institutional Failures: Sutter Health

Documented Failure

Serial imaging studies failed to recognize or communicate progressive aortic arch tortuosity and connective tissue fragility, missing critical interdisciplinary planning opportunities.

Downstream Harm

  • Delayed interdisciplinary planning for structural intervention
  • Severe localized muscle and nerve entrapment from structural changes
  • Increased risk of acute vascular events due to unmonitored changes

Required Remediation

  • Internal case review integration and forensic radiology review
  • Radiology staff retraining on connective tissue disorders
  • Implementation of structured reporting for serial imaging studies
