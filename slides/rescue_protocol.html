Emergency Response: The 3-Step Rescue Protocol

STEP 1: Terminate Exposure (Immediate)

Stop all infusions; halt procedures. Disconnect IV lines; flush with normal saline. Activate emergency response.

STEP 2: Enzymatic Bypass (Within 30 Minutes)

IV NAC: 1000 mg bolus, then 500 mg IV q4h for 24h. | IM Methyl-B12: 1000 mcg immediately, repeat q12h x 48h. Force open methylation pathways.

STEP 3: Mineral Restoration (Concurrent)

IV Magnesium Sulfate: 1-2 g over 30-60 min. | IV Potassium: 20-40 mEq over 1-2h (if low). Stabilize cell membrane potential.

Post-Rescue Monitoring & Escalation

Continuous cardiac monitoring. Repeat electrolytes q2h x 12h, then q4h x 48h. Immediate escalation to Dr. Jessica Eby's team and UCLA Patient Affairs.
