# DHCS NEMT Technical Codes & PCP Form Requirements

## 1. Primary NEMT Procedure Codes (HCPCS)
These are the codes the PCP must use on the **Physician Certification Statement (PCS)** and the **Treatment Authorization Request (TAR)**:

| Code | Description | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **A0130** | Non-emergency transportation: wheelchair van | Requires TAR |
| **A0120** | Non-emergency transportation: mini-bus, mountain area transport, or other specialized vehicle | Requires TAR |
| **A0425** | Ground mileage, per statute mile | Requires TAR |
| **T2003** | Non-emergency transportation; encounter/trip | Requires TAR |

## 2. TAR (Treatment Authorization Request) Requirements
- **Form:** DHCS 18-1 (Treatment Authorization Request)
- **Mandatory Attachment:** A completed **Physician Certification Statement (PCS)** form.
- **PCP Responsibility:** The PCP must sign the PCS form attesting that the member's medical and physical condition does not allow them to travel by bus, taxi, or other forms of public or private transportation.

## 3. 50-Mile Geographical Exemption
- **Regulation:** CCR Title 22, Section 51008.
- **Rule:** If a patient is traveling more than **50 miles** from their residence to a facility (e.g., 1000-mile transit), the PCP must provide a **Geographical Exemption** justification.
- **Justification Language:** "The medically necessary services required by the patient are not available within 50 miles of the patient's residence. The specific expertise for [Genomic/PHP1b/Lynch] management is only available at [UCLA Health]."

## 4. Multi-Trip Signature & Stamp Mandate
- **PCP Requirement:** For members with recurring appointments, the PCP can sign a **Multi-Trip Voucher**.
- **Mandatory Elements:**
    1. **Original Signature** in blue or black ink.
    2. **Provider Stamp** or printed name/address/NPI.
    3. **Date of Service** for each trip.
    4. **NPI Number** of the signing provider.

## 5. STD 204 (Payee Data Record)
- **Purpose:** Required by the State of California to process payments to non-governmental entities.
- **Requirement:** The vendor (transportation provider) or the member (if seeking direct reimbursement) must have a valid STD 204 on file with DHCS.

## 6. Provider Non-Compliance Reporting
- If a PCP refuses to sign a PCS/TAR for a documented impairment, this constitutes a **denial of access to care**.
- **Escalation:** Report to **DHCS Office of the Ombudsman** at 1-888-452-8609.
