The **polygenic risk for Thyroid Cancer** [MyHeritage Health Report] also mandates vigilant endocrine and oncology surveillance for early detection and intervention.

This leads to a buildup of harmful compounds, exacerbating systemic inflammation, oxidative stress, and directly hindering DNA repair mechanisms already compromised by the PMS2 deletion. This also profoundly impacts neurotransmitter synthesis and degradation, as well as the critical conversion of **Vitamin D and B vitamins**, further compromising cellular function and overall systemic health. This profound methylation dysfunction is a central culprit in the systemic contortion and osteos observed, as it directly impacts bone remodeling, connective tissue integrity, and the body's ability to repair and maintain its structural framework. The **pharmacogenomic implications** [dr. update ucd .docx] of this methylation blockade are critical, as it directly influences the body's ability to process drugs and chemicals, necessitating careful consideration of all pharmaceutical and chemical exposures during pre-procedure planning and treatment.

*   **Estrogen Metabolite Dysregulation:** The methylation impairment directly impacts estrogen metabolism, leading to a buildup of problematic estrogen metabolites. [EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION_NOTES.md, 71-126]

*   **Methyl Donors & Vitamin D/B Conversion Disorders:** The Genova panel highlights specific deficiencies in methyl donors and impaired conversion of essential B vitamins and Vitamin D.
