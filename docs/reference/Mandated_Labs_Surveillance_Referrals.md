## **I. General Principles for All Medical Interventions**

1.  **Genomically-Informed Care:** All diagnostic and therapeutic interventions must be cross-verified against the patient's complete genetic profile (Variantyx, Invitae, Tempus, MyHeritage) and methylation status (Genova) prior to care authorization. Standard clinical pathways that assume normal G-protein transduction, detoxification, or immune function are contraindicated.
2.  **HRT Contraindication:** Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) is absolutely contraindicated due to documented hormone signaling impairment, detoxification blockade, and history of neuro-collapse. Any attempt to administer HRT constitutes medical negligence.
3.  **G6PD Deficiency Precautions:** All medical interventions, dietary recommendations, and lifestyle advice must explicitly consider potential triggers for hemolytic crises. Medications, foods, and environmental exposures must be screened for their hemolytic potential.
4.  **Methylation Status:** The severe methylation dysfunction documented by Genova (February 2024) is a validated physiological reality and must be incorporated into all care decisions, particularly regarding detoxification, medication metabolism, and nutrient support.
5.  **ADA & Patient Rights:** All care must adhere to ADA accommodations and patient rights, including medically justified refusals of contraindicated procedures or substances (e.g., PEG 3350 / MiraLAX).
6.  **Provider Competency:** Treating providers must demonstrate competency in understanding and managing the patient's overlapping conditions, including Lynch Syndrome, PHP1b, ALPS, neurovascular risks, and detoxification impairment.

### **C. Cardiovascular & Neurovascular Monitoring**

**Nuclear Medicine & Contrast Needs:** All nuclear medicine studies and procedures requiring contrast must adhere to specific protocols due to the patient's severe methylation dysfunction (Genova, Feb 2024), MTHFR/COMT variants, ABCB1 dysfunction, and G6PD deficiency. Protocols must include:
*   **Pre-medication:** Mandatory Benadryl pre-medication for genetic reactions.
*   **Contrast Type:** Use of specific contrast agents with known safety profiles for detoxification-impaired individuals.
*   **Dosage & Frequency:** Minimized dosage and extended washout periods where appropriate.
*   **Monitoring:** Enhanced post-procedure monitoring for adverse reactions and metabolite buildup.
