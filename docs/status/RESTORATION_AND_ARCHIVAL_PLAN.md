# Medical Advocacy Hub — Restoration and Legacy-Page Plan

## Decision status

**No visual or route change in this document has been applied.** This plan corrects the earlier mistake of reducing the established system to a color adjustment. It uses observed structural patterns from the referenced Universal Changemakers, Black Plum Concierge, A Moonlit Room, and Eternal Autumn sites, while keeping those entities’ content separate from the medical-advocacy site.

## What the observed system actually does

The reference sites are not dashboard interfaces. They use an **editorial, cinematic, and document-led composition**: a restrained header, an immersive first field, one primary route plus one secondary route, clear negative space, serif-forward display hierarchy, small tracked operational labels, thin dividers, numbered progressions, and content that moves in deliberate bands rather than repeated equal-weight cards. See `VISUAL_SYSTEM_DISCOVERY.md` for the observed evidence.

The medical site should translate that behavior into a **private evidence-file experience**, not duplicate any UCM, Eternal Autumn, Black Plum, or Moonlit Room business language, entity structure, imagery, or programs.

The following classification is a **content-control proposal**, not a claim that the records are invalid. A route may contain valuable history but still be inappropriate as an active site page until source and clinician review are complete.

|---|---|---|

Please identify which visual reference governs the **medical site’s composition**:

1. **A Moonlit Room** — cinematic source-file composition, sparse header, editorial pathways.
2. **Black Plum Concierge** — quieter concierge intake, centered hierarchy, restrained sequence.
3. **Eternal Autumn** — immersive atmospheric narrative with larger visual fields.
4. **A specific other site or page** — provide its URL or screenshot.

After that one decision, the implementation will: preserve the current site as a rollback checkpoint, rebuild the active evidence pages in the approved composition, and move—not delete—the listed legacy routes into a controlled archive.
