# Content Merge & Revision Protocol

**Project:** `medical-advocacy-hub`  
**Date:** August 13, 2026  
**Status:** Staging & Revision Hub  

## Purpose
This protocol governs the intake, verification, and integration of external site details and historical chat artifacts into the master medical advocacy architecture.

## Core Rules for Merging Content
1. **Genomic Anchor Compliance:** All incoming clinical narratives or departmental protocols must be cross-verified against confirmed genetic findings (`STX16` deletion / `PHP1b`, `PMS2` deletion / Lynch Syndrome, and methylation bottlenecks).
2. **Quarantine Enforcement:** Outdated emergency protocols (such as generalized saline administration during acute neuro-crash events) remain strictly quarantined and excluded from active deployment.
3. **Stylistic Consistency:** All merged sections must adhere to the Nocturnal Luxury aesthetic (deep plum-black background, cream text, muted rose-plum accents) and avoid saturated red-on-blue or unreadable color contrasts.
4. **Traceability:** Every integrated record must cite its source file or verifiable clinical log within the project evidence vault.
