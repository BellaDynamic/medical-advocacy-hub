# Care-Coordination Transcript Ledger

## Source and use boundary

**Source:** `Pasted_content_02.txt`, supplied by the user.  
**Classification:** Patient-experience and care-coordination transcript.  
**Permitted use:** Preserve reported access barriers, reported requests, and the list of source documents needed to verify or resolve them.  
**Not permitted:** Treat the transcript as a final clinical finding, insurance decision, signed order, radiology interpretation, legal conclusion, or substantiated allegation.

| Review lane | Reported topic | Original records needed before a site conclusion | Suggested review owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medication access | Reported mismatch among pharmacy, payer, and portal information regarding a GI-related prescription and authorization paperwork. | Prescription/order, pharmacy rejection or claim response, submitted authorization request, payer decision, provider communication. | Prescriber, pharmacy, payer authorization unit, coordinator. |
| Imaging and results | Reported uncertainty about the ordered study scope, who will discuss results, and responsibility following a provider transition. | Signed order, final report, image-access record, referral note, documented clinician follow-up. | Ordering clinician, radiology records, receiving clinician, coordinator. |
| Transfer and referrals | Reported reassignment/transition concerns, messaging-access barriers, and request for a bridge plan. | Current care-team assignment, transfer note, active referrals, appointment/contact history, written plan. | Primary-care office, receiving clinician, referral team. |
| Travel and specialty access | Reported recurring travel and a request for transportation authorization and coordinated specialty scheduling. | Current coverage, policy/form, appointment schedule, provider certification if applicable, payer determination. | Payer transportation unit, specialty office, primary care. |
| Tissue/lesion concern | Reported prior excision history and current concern regarding chest or axillary tissue changes. | Operative report, pathology report, final imaging, current clinician assessment, referral, treating-team plan. | Treating clinician, clinically appropriate specialty, radiology, pathology records. |

## Evidence boundary

Statements in the transcript about disease mechanisms, genetic inheritance, medication safety, imaging completeness, provider intent, payer fault, discrimination, negligence, or legal outcomes require controlling records and qualified review. The transcript should remain linked as context rather than overwritten or elevated to proof.
