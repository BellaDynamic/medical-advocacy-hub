## Key Principles

✅ **Source-led:** Every claim is tied to a document, record, or verified communication

✅ **Legally defensible:** Distinguishes confirmed facts from reported concerns from items needing verification

✅ **Chronological:** Events are dated and sequenced to show causation

✅ **Institutional accountability:** Clear mapping of which provider/facility had duty to act and where they failed

✅ **Financial transparency:** Damages quantified and documented

✅ **Government-ready:** Structured for regulatory complaints, audits, and administrative action

## Confidentiality & Use

This archive contains **sensitive personal medical information, genetic data, legal strategy, and recorded communications.** 

- Keep original records secure and encrypted
- Remove identifying information (names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, account numbers) from working copies
- Share only with authorized attorneys, medical experts, regulators, or legal representatives
- Do not post identifying medical records publicly
- Preserve chain of custody for all evidence
